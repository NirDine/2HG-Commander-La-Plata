What is a Dungeon?
Dungeons is a community ran archenemy-style format inspired by Hearthstone's Dungeon Runs, where a team of 2-3 players face off against a series of themed enemy decks.

How does a Dungeon work?

You play on a team

Each team consists of 2-3 players. You can bring some friends or play with new people.

You're given a deck

Choose a color and a deck will be provided for you. Don't worry, you get to upgrade that deck as you face new enemies!

Defeat all four villains

Face off against stronger foes as you progress thorugh the dungeon.
